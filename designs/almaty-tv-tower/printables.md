**Gotcha hit this time:** the big "Drag your files here" drop zone looks like it does nothing, but a `file_upload` into it DOES land in the Model Files section (just not shown up top). Uploading twice (thinking the first failed) created a full duplicate set — had to select the 6 duplicates and "Delete 6 files". So: after one upload, scroll down to the Model Files section to confirm before re-uploading. Upload photos straight into the Photos `+` input and model files into the Model Files input to avoid the confusing drop zone.
