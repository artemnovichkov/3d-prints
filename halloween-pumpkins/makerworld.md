## Description

A pumpkin that talks with its shadow. 🎃 Shine a light from the left and the wall says **BOO**. Move the light to the right and it says **EEK**. The pumpkin itself shows no letters, just stripes.

👻 **Four pumpkins, eight words**
- BOO / EEK
- BAT / CAT
- RIP / BYE
- OMG / RUN

🔦 **How to use**
1. Thread a thin string in through one stem hole and out through the other, so the pumpkin hangs flat along a garland.
2. Hang the pumpkin 5–10 cm from a wall, in a dark room.
3. Light it from the side with a phone flashlight or a candle, at least 40 cm away and about 30° to the side.
4. Move the light to the other side and the word flips.

🧠 **How it works**
Each pumpkin is a grid of thin slats split lengthwise. Inside a word one half of each slat is taller and closes the gap next to it under slanted light, so the word turns solid black. Light from the left and from the right catch different halves, so each side gets its own word.

🖨️ **Printing**
- All four on one plate: 44 min, 9.6 g of PLA on a P2S.
- 76 × 62 mm, 1.6 mm thick. 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, no supports, no AMS, one colour.
- Print flat. Arachne walls are on in the 3MF for the thin slat halves. A clean first layer matters.

✏️ **Make your own words**
Open it in Parametric Model Maker and type your own two words. Three or four capitals read best; longer words shrink to fit the pumpkin. Under Hanging, keep two small holes for a garland string or switch to one bigger hole for a hook or nail.
