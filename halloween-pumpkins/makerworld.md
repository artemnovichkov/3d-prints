## Description

A pumpkin that talks with its shadow. 🎃 Shine a light from the left and the wall says **BOO**. Move the light to the right and it says **EEK**. The pumpkin itself shows no letters, just stripes.

👻 **Four pumpkins, eight words**
- BOO / EEK
- BAT / CAT
- RIP / BYE
- OMG / RUN

🔦 **How to use**
1. Thread a string through the hole in the stem.
2. Hang the pumpkin 5–10 cm from a wall, in a dark room.
3. Light it from the side with a phone flashlight or a candle, at least 40 cm away and about 30° to the side.
4. Move the light to the other side and the word flips.

🧠 **How it works**
Each pumpkin is a grid of thin slats split lengthwise. Inside a word one half of each slat is taller and closes the gap next to it under slanted light, so the word turns solid black. Light from the left and from the right catch different halves, so each side gets its own word. Away from the words the slats are single thin lines, so the rest of the shadow stays bright.

🖨️ **Printing**
- All four on one plate: 41 min, 7.7 g of PLA on a P2S.
- 76 × 62 mm, 1.6 mm thick. 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, no supports, no AMS, one colour.
- Print flat. Arachne walls are on in the 3MF, so the 0.5 mm slats print as single lines. A clean first layer matters.
